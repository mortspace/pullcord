Ruling

One number rules a button: the glyph size G. Everything else is a fixed multiple of it, so every button in a tier is the same height, radius and width, and the label sits in the same place.

  • Grid. Art is always 9×9 cells. Cell pitch is G ÷ 9, the gap is 26% of a cell, cell radius is 15% of a cell. Bigger grids only add padding around the same art.
  • Glyph. A G×G box, flush left inside the button, vertically centered.
  • Label. Inter 600, size 0.52 G, line-height 1, letter-spacing −1.5%, left-aligned, vertically centered on the glyph. One line; it never wraps.
  • Button. Height 1.6 G. Left padding 0.45 G, gap to label 0.4 G, right padding 0.6 G. Radius 0.5 G. Width is fixed at 8.5 G so a row of buttons lines up and a 22-character label fits; switch to Fit for natural width.
  • Color. Three stops per hue: bed at 16% alpha, lit at full saturation, white-hot. Button fill is pure black in dark mode and pure white in light mode.
